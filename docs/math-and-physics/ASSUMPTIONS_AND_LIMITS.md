## What each family assumes

| Family | Included | Excluded or unresolved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Planning | Ordered demand segments, assumed PUE, illustrative/unknown energy-only rates | Supply continuity, delivered compute service, demand charges, CAPEX, taxes and calibrated cooling |
| Continuity | One aggregate IT load, nonnegative gross power flow, finite storage, fixed efficiencies, discrete availability/domain events and deterministic recovery | AC load flow, impedance sharing, switching/protection transients, battery aging/temperature, generator ramp/cooldown/fuel, probabilistic reliability, cooling/workload queues |
| Infrastructure screens | Explicit scalar equations, input/output units, source labels, selected golden cases and review states | Equipment selection, compliance, detailed grounding/protection/CFD/structural/network engineering; Engineering HOLD persists |
| EMT | Ideal DC source, linear R-L/C/resistor circuit, fixed-step RK4 and signed energy | Semiconductor switching, rectifier/UPS controllers, clamps, reverse-blocking devices, nonlinear component limits, AC network or thermal physics |
| Research | Adapted reduced equations, bounded synthetic inputs, recomputed initialization, guarded modal/response calculations | Missing MATLAB originals, missing measured GPU trace, independently validated physical parameters or facility calibration |
| Offline companion | Synthetic utilization wave, algebraic cooling and heuristic inlet relaxation | Native outage dispatch, UPS depletion, server reboot behavior, chiller cascades or measured annual PUE |

## Numerical agreement has a defined scope

The continuity invariant is exact inside a rational model. Its exported zero residual is not a physical sensor reading. The separately reconstructed finite-decimal ledger tolerates serialization rounding. The TypeScript/Python parity tests compare independently implemented software for stated fixtures; shared assumptions can still be wrong. A passing equation check for a teaching screen does not establish design suitability. [docs/validation/evidence-method.md](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/docs/validation/evidence-method.md), [apps/web/tests-engine/engine.test.ts](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/apps/web/tests-engine/engine.test.ts), [scripts/verify_infrastructure.py](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/scripts/verify_infrastructure.py).

EMT tolerances are numerical comparisons and refinement checks. Its endpoints can miss a continuous-time extremum; its ideal source can absorb energy and its ideal capacitor voltage can become negative. Replacing those results with clipped values would alter the model, not correct a documentation issue. [datacenter_twin/emt.py](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/datacenter_twin/emt.py), [apps/web/src/emt/engine.ts](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/apps/web/src/emt/engine.ts).

Research acceptance checks recompute residuals and reject nonfinite/ill-conditioned results. They do not prove global convergence, uniqueness, physical realizability or a domain outside the exposed parameter bounds. The mode flag includes real parts up to $10^{-8}$, so it must not be paraphrased as strict asymptotic stability. Frequency-response peaks are sampled on a finite grid. The model comparison retains startup transients and two different representations; disagreement is not automatically an error in one model. [datacenter_twin/research/sdcib.py](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/datacenter_twin/research/sdcib.py), [datacenter_twin/research/model_comparison.py](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/datacenter_twin/research/model_comparison.py), [datacenter_twin/research/grid_network.py](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/datacenter_twin/research/grid_network.py).

## Interface and data assumptions

Input validation has several layers. Python JSON parsing rejects duplicate keys and has a 4 MiB boundary; browser `JSON.parse` does not supply the same duplicate-key rule. The file picker permits a 10 MiB read before revalidation and later request-size checks. Typed declarations do not replace runtime validation. A source parse is not a typecheck, and a native Node test is not a browser journey. [datacenter_twin/contracts.py](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/datacenter_twin/contracts.py), [apps/web/src/javascript-worker.ts](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/apps/web/src/javascript-worker.ts), [apps/web/src/ScenarioFiles.tsx](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/apps/web/src/ScenarioFiles.tsx).

`it_capacity_kw` is a reported IT envelope; gross path/node capacities constrain dispatch. Generator startup follows the utility asset's availability, not every downstream loss of service. Same-time event order is meaningful. The charging-enabled CLI preset and charging-disabled guided lesson must not share a claimed input identity. [datacenter_twin/continuity.py](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/datacenter_twin/continuity.py), [docs/canonical-case.md](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/docs/canonical-case.md).

`calculateScreen('releaseGate', ...)` accepts six review-state strings. The generic `buildScreenResult` wrapper first clones numeric lesson inputs and rejects those strings. That is a documented existing wrapper limitation, not a failure of the current twelve numeric infrastructure lessons. No source repair is included in this documentation package. [apps/web/src/infrastructure/screens.ts:679–717](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/apps/web/src/infrastructure/screens.ts#L679-L717), [apps/web/src/infrastructure/screens.ts:796–838](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/apps/web/src/infrastructure/screens.ts#L796-L838).

The bundled hardware/offering/model catalog is dated provenance and explicit assumptions. It does not fetch live prices, certify a vendor claim, convert node/GPU/token billing into equivalent throughput, or supply a calibrated jurisdictional cost model. `null` values remain unknown. [datacenter_twin/catalog.py](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/datacenter_twin/catalog.py), [datacenter_twin/calculations.py](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/datacenter_twin/calculations.py).

## Evidence and rights

Original software and synthetic fixtures retain Apache-2.0 terms and notices. Imported runtime/vendor materials retain their separate notices. The research adaptation identifies its supplied archive; absent originals remain absent. Infrastructure lesson labels distinguish source statements, editorial derivations, illustrative assumptions and unresolved material. The documented educational publication decision does not remove Engineering HOLD, grant new third-party rights or make the private manuscript distributable. [NOTICE](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/NOTICE), [docs/third-party/README.md](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/docs/third-party/README.md), [docs/releases/v2.0.0-publication-decision.md](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/blob/d8dc3773830a397409a7a9c00a6ffed33c46fb4b/docs/releases/v2.0.0-publication-decision.md).

No facility measurement dataset, external independent reproduction, FAT/SAT, physical safety assessment, certification, service-level/Tier result or real equipment control is established by this audit. A later calibration project requires its own data rights, units, measurement uncertainty, validation owner and accepted use boundary. Existing public tutorials and historical evidence remain intact; the new roadmap labels such future work as conditional proposals.
