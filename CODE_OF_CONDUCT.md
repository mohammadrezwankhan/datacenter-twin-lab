# Code of Conduct

Datacenter Twin Lab is a technical learning and research project. People may disagree about assumptions, algorithms, documentation, or scope; discussion must remain useful, specific, and respectful.

Participants are expected to:

- describe evidence, units, revisions, and uncertainty when making technical claims;
- welcome good-faith questions from people with different levels of electrical, Python, or research-software experience;
- focus criticism on the work and its assumptions;
- protect private information and respect third-party data rights; and
- leave room for correction when a result or interpretation changes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or sexualized conduct, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of credentials or private data, spam, impersonation, and knowingly misleading claims about facility safety, calibration, certification, or project endorsement.

For a public, non-confidential concern, open a focused issue or use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/datacenter-twin-lab/discussions) when enabled. For a confidential security report, use the route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Include only the minimum information needed to explain the concern. Project maintainers may close or limit participation in material that violates this code or prevents a productive technical exchange.
